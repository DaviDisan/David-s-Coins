About David

I'm David DiSanto, and David's Coins & Collectibles is my business. I run it myself from Katy, Texas, and sell online and by mail.

Every coin on this site is one I own. I photograph each one front and back on the same table, under the same light, so what you see on the inventory page is the exact coin you'd get, not a stock image.

Before anything is listed or bought, raw coins are weighed on a 0.1 g scale and looked over under a digital microscope. Certified coins stay in their original PCGS, NGC, ANACS or ICG holders.

When you fill out a form or send an email, it comes straight to me, and I answer it myself.
