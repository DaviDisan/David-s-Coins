Appraisals

If you just want to know what something is worth, send me photos and I'll give you a free estimate. There's no obligation to sell.

How I evaluate a coin

Raw coins go on a 0.1 g scale first, since weight is the quickest way to catch a fake or a coin with the wrong metal content. Then I look at the surfaces under a digital microscope for wear, cleaning, tooling and die varieties that change a coin's value.

From photos I can usually give you a solid range. For a firm number, I'll need the coin in hand.

For insurance or an estate

If you need the values written down for insurance, an estate or dividing a collection, ask and I can put it in writing.
