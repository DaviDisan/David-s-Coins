Free Online Appraisals

How selling to us works

The whole process happens online and by mail, with no appointments and no pressure.

  1. 01

    Send us photos

    Use the inquiry form or email clear photos of the front and back. Please don't clean your coins, since cleaning can sharply reduce their value.

  2. 02

    Get a clear offer

    We review the grade, weight, and metal content and send you a written quote tied to the live spot price.

  3. 03

    Ship & get paid

    Accept the offer and we'll send shipping instructions. Once your coins arrive and are verified with calibrated scales and a digital microscope, you get paid.
