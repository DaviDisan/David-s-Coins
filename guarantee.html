My guarantee

Two promises come with everything you buy from me.

30-day money-back guarantee

If you're not happy with a purchase for any reason, send it back within 30 days and I'll refund your money.

Lifetime authenticity guarantee

Every coin I sell is guaranteed genuine, with no time limit. If you ever have any reason to suspect a coin you bought from me isn't authentic, send it back and I'll refund you in full, whether that's next week or years from now.

How I check coins

Raw coins are weighed on a 0.1 g scale and examined under a digital microscope before I list them. Certified coins stay in their original PCGS, NGC, ANACS or ICG holders.
