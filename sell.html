Selling your coins

I buy U.S. and world coins, silver and gold bullion, proof and mint sets, and certified slabs, from single coins to whole collections.

What I pay

For bullion, the offer starts from the live spot price. For collector coins, it comes down to date, mint mark, grade and current demand, and I'll walk you through how I got to the number instead of just handing you one.

How it works

Send me clear photos of both sides through the form or by email, with dates and quantities if you have them. Please don't clean anything first; cleaning can wipe out most of a coin's collector value.

I'll reply with a written offer. If you accept, I'll send shipping instructions, and you're paid once the coins arrive and check out.

Estates and large collections

If you've inherited a collection or have more than you can photograph, send a rough list of what's there and we'll work out the easiest way to go through it.
